Project Journey - Step by Step

Key Learnings & Insights

Important Findings
  1. Synthetic vs Real Economic Data: Surprisingly, synthetic economic data (V3: 56.86%) outperformed real FRED/BEA data (V4: 47.99%). This demonstrates that well-engineered synthetic features can capture market patterns better than noisy real-world economic indicators.
  2. Feature Engineering Matters: Expanding from 83 to 91 features improved accuracy significantly. The additional lag features and interaction terms provided crucial predictive power.
  3. High Confidence = High Accuracy: Predictions with >70% confidence achieved 72.73% accuracy on backtest and 65.62% on live data, validating the confidence scoring system.
  4. News Sentiment Impact: While news sentiment alone showed weak correlation (-0.0521), when combined with technical and economic features, it significantly improved predictions.
  5. Model Consistency: The model maintained similar performance across training (56.86%), backtest (63.64%), and live predictions (62.30%), indicating good generalization without overfitting.
